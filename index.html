Hermès · Retail Activation

Le Monde d'Hermès Kiosk

A traveling activation that turns a store opening into a destination — installed market by market, from Aspen to Austin.

The Le Monde d'Hermès kiosk is a recurring activation format built for store openings across the U.S. — 2024–2025 markets included Aspen, Seattle, Princeton, Los Angeles, the Meatpacking District/Brooklyn, and Nashville. Each stop is scoped to its market: astonishment as the guiding theme for Austin's launch, an evening built around a lit kiosk and a panel series for Aspen's.

I built the 2024–2025 market strategy and ran point on activation — from the kiosk's role in each opening to what "success" needed to look like, city by city.

  • Guests, Austin launch6,500
  • Guests, Aspen recap1,300+
  • Aspen activation budget$550,500
  • Earned media value, Aspen$18.7K

Hermès · Store Openings, 2020–2026

Artist Barricades

Fifteen-plus store openings, each paired with a commissioned artist — turning construction walls into original work before the doors even open.

Every new Hermès store starts behind a barricade. This program turns that construction wall into a commission: a local artist, briefed and paired with the market, given the wall as a canvas ahead of opening day.

Artists across the program include Clark Richert (Aventura & Short Hills), Ellen Rutt (Troy), Damin Lujan (Costa Mesa), Sophie Roach & Laura Lit (Austin), Mike Perry (706 Madison, NYC), Math Bass (Topanga Canyon), Dahlia Elsayed (Princeton), Magalie Guerin (Chicago), Vanna Black (Atlanta), Danielle Hacche (Scottsdale), Tess Davies (Nashville), and Liz Flores (Plaza del Lago).

I sourced the artists, wrote the brief each one worked from, and kept the calendar — barricade install to ribbon-cutting — across every market on the list.

Hermès · Out-of-Home

Meatpacking Billboard

One mural unit above 14th Street, recast for every campaign flight that comes through the district.

The M-62 unit sits above the Meatpacking District — a single mural surface that gets a new creative treatment with each campaign: Holiday 2025, February and May 2026, and a tie-in wrap for Mystery at the Grooms'.

I managed the vendor relationship and creative rotation, from proof-of-performance documentation to keeping the unit in step with whatever the brand was running that season.

Hermès · Immersive Event & Film

Mystery in the Making

“Mystery at the Grooms'” — a citywide immersive event and the film that came out of it, from Shanghai to a pier on the East River.

Mystery at the Grooms' took the brand's equestrian world and turned it into a scavenger-hunt-style immersive event — first in Shanghai, then in New York at Pier 36, June 19–29, 2025. The city got the campaign too: a hand-painted Meatpacking takeover, a Pier 36 venue wrap, an East Broadway subway takeover, a New York Times cover wrap, and a coloring-page tie-in with The New Yorker.

I worked the run of show — rehearsals through the public sessions, press brunch, and VIP dinners — and helped produce the recap film, interviewing Diane Mahady (President & CEO, Hermès Americas) and Charlotte David (VP Communications) along the way, on a $75,000 production budget.

About

It's the thread running through six years at Hermès, from media coordinator to media manager, always closest to the moments where a brand becomes something people want to stop and look at.
